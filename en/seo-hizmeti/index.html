SEO Service — Being Found in Search Engines

SEO (search engine optimisation) is the technical and content work that helps your site get found for relevant searches on engines such as Google. We do not promise rankings; we make measurable improvements and report clearly what changed.

We start with a technical audit of the current site: page titles, descriptions, heading structure, speed, mobile friendliness, sitemap and internal links. Then we build a keyword and content plan and prepare local-search (Gaziantep) recommendations including the Google Business Profile.

SEO is ongoing work and pays off together with regular maintenance and content.

What we do

  • Technical SEO audit and fixes
  • Keyword and content plan
  • Page title, description and structured-data setup
  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile recommendations
  • Speed and mobile improvements
  • Regular reporting
