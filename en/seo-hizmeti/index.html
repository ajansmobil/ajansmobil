SEO Service in Gaziantep — Being Found in Search Engines

Ajans Mobil is a digital agency in Gaziantep, Turkey, doing technical and content work to help websites get found on search engines such as Google. We do not promise rankings; we make measurable improvements and report clearly what changed.

What is SEO?

SEO (search engine optimisation) is the whole of the technical adjustments and content work that help your site get found for relevant searches. The aim is for the right people to reach the right page; we measure not only position but also the enquiries that arrive by phone and WhatsApp.

What do we do?

  • Technical SEO audit: page titles, descriptions, heading structure, site speed, mobile fit, sitemap and internal links.
  • Keyword and content plan: finding the phrases your customers really search for and the topics competitors cover that you lack.
  • Structured data: JSON-LD markup for your business, services and frequently asked questions that search engines and AI assistants can understand.
  • Local SEO: consistent address and phone details and Google Business Profile suggestions for Gaziantep searches.
  • Reporting: regular tracking of position and visibility changes with Search Console data.

Preparing for AI search (GEO)

People now ask their questions to AI assistants too. With a clear entity definition, question-and-answer blocks and an llms.txt file we try to raise the chance that your site is mentioned in those answers.

How we work

  1. Measurement: measuring your current site, competitors and search positions.
  2. Priority list: ordering high-impact, easy-to-apply tasks.
  3. Implementation: technical fixes and content production.
  4. Follow-up: regular measurement; work with no visible effect is reconsidered.

SEO is ongoing work; it gains meaning together with regular maintenance and content production.
